Informations

The expanded Morski Shopping Centre offers a wide range of food, furniture, home furnishings, and renovation and building materials.

The hypermarket, service outlets and food and drink establishments provide the opportunity for a comprehensive and enjoyable shopping experience. The centre is situated near the rapidly developing residential estates of Gdańsk Południe, whilst its proximity to the Tri-City ring road makes it easily accessible to customers from neighbouring towns. The extensive grounds on which Morski Park Handlowy is situated, the large number of free parking spaces and the absence of architectural barriers make the centre an ideal shopping destination.
