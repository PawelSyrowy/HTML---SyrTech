The expanded Morski Shopping Centre offers a wide range of food, furniture, home furnishings, and renovation and building materials.

The hypermarket, service outlets and food and drink establishments provide the opportunity for a comprehensive and enjoyable shopping experience. The centre is situated near the rapidly developing residential estates of Gdańsk Południe.

Its proximity to the Tri-City ring road makes it easily accessible to customers from neighbouring towns. The extensive grounds and the large number of free parking spaces make the centre an ideal shopping destination.
